How does it work?

One asset in.
A verified package out.

You explain what you meant to make. Asset Shepherd measures the GLB, asks about consequential changes, and proves what came out.

  1. 01
    Describe the target

    The agent proposes the intended use and real-world size from your words. You confirm or adjust it once.

  2. 02
    Upload the untouched GLB

    Deterministic tools measure scale, orientation, grounding, names, geometry, materials, textures, and structural risks.

  3. 03
    Make one meaningful decision

    Safe display-name fixes can run automatically. Physical normalization waits for your explicit approval.

  4. 04
    Download with proof

    The candidate is independently re-inspected and packaged with the findings, plan, decision, verification, and provenance.

The safety boundary

Your original stays untouched. The agent cannot approve its own physical changes.

What can Asset Shepherd repair?

Version 1 repairs node and mesh display names, plus an approved reversible root normalization for scale, upright orientation, and grounding. It reports—but does not alter—topology, UVs, materials, textures, rigs, or animation.
