What this choice changes

Choose the ordinary use case, not a polygon count. Asset Shepherd translates it into a mesh-complexity guideline and may propose a reversible simplification when the source exceeds it.

  1. Hero / close-upUp to 50,000 triangles for assets players may inspect closely.
  2. Normal gameplayUp to 15,000 triangles for ordinary gameplay distance.
  3. Background / repeatedUp to 2,500 triangles when an asset is distant, small on screen, or appears many times.

If the source is already below the guideline, Asset Shepherd leaves it alone. If optimization is proposed, you can decline it and preserve the original detail.
